+ Were they slaves?
Modern archaeology has overturned the popular image of pyramid-builders as slaves. Excavated workers' villages show paid laborers who received medical care, meat rations, and burial with honor. Graffiti found in "relieving chambers" above the King's Chamber includes gang names like "Friends of Khufu" — suggesting workers who took pride in their labor. It was state-organized work, likely tied to a religious duty to participate in the pharaoh's eternal life.

In a cave in the Dordogne valley of France, prehistoric humans paint over 600 animals — bulls, horses, stags, ibex — using ochre, manganese, charcoal, and hematite.
