II — The Movement

A rotor of 18 karat gold, winding in both directions

The Perpetual rotor — an invention Rolex patented in 1931 — converts the kinetic energy of the wearer's wrist into stored mechanical power. On the Calibre 3235, the bidirectional oscillating weight is crafted from solid 18 karat gold and never requires manual winding under typical wear conditions.
